# Password Reset

## How to reset your password

1. Go to the login page and click **"Forgot password"**.
2. Enter the email address linked to your account.
3. You will receive a reset link by email within **5 minutes**.
4. Click the link and choose a new password (at least 8 characters, with one number).

## Reset email not arriving

- Check your **spam and junk** folders first.
- Make sure you typed the correct email address — the one you registered with.
- Add `support@example-shop.com` to your contacts so our emails are not blocked.
- If it still does not arrive after 15 minutes, contact support and we can reset your password manually after verifying your identity.

## Locked out of your account

- After 5 failed login attempts your account is locked for **30 minutes**.
- Wait 30 minutes, then use the password reset link to get back in.
