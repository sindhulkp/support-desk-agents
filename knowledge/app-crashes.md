# App Crashes and Errors

## App crashes on checkout

1. First, update the app to the latest version from your app store.
2. Clear the app cache: **Settings → Apps → Our App → Clear Cache**.
3. Restart your phone and try again.
4. If it still crashes, uninstall and reinstall the app — your account and orders are saved on our servers, so you will not lose anything.

## Website error messages

- If you see an error page, try refreshing and clearing your browser cache.
- Try a different browser, or open the site in a private/incognito window.

## Still not working?

- Contact support with your phone model, app version, and a screenshot of the error if you have one.
- As a workaround, you can always place your order on our website instead of the app.
