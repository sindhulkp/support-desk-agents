# Refund Policy

## How refunds work

- You can return any unused item within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.
- Refunds are sent to the original payment method.
- Once your return is received at our warehouse, the refund is processed within **5–10 business days**.
- You will get an email confirmation as soon as the refund is issued.

## Double charges

- If you were charged twice for the same order or subscription, contact support with the order number and the last 4 digits of your card.
- We will verify the duplicate charge and refund the extra amount within **3–5 business days**.

## What cannot be refunded

- Items returned after 30 days are not eligible for a refund, but you may receive store credit at our discretion.
- Gift cards and final-sale items cannot be refunded.
