# Damaged Item

## What to do if your item arrives damaged

1. Take photos of the damaged item and the packaging.
2. Contact support within **7 days** of delivery with your order number and the photos.
3. We will offer a **free replacement** or a **full refund** — your choice.

## How replacements work

- The replacement is shipped free of charge as soon as we confirm the damage.
- You do not need to return the damaged item unless we ask you to.
- Replacement delivery takes **3–5 business days**.

## What if the box is dented but the item is fine?

- If the item itself works fine, you can keep it. If you are not happy, you can still return it within the 30-day return window for a full refund.
