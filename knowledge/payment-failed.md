# Payment Failed

## Why a payment fails

- The most common reasons are an expired card, insufficient funds, or a wrong billing address.
- Some banks block online payments by default — you may need to approve the payment in your banking app.

## What to do

1. Check that your card is not expired and the billing address matches your bank records.
2. Try a different card or payment method.
3. If the payment still fails, contact your bank and ask whether the transaction was blocked.

## Charged but order not confirmed

- If money left your account but you did not get an order confirmation, do not pay again.
- Contact support with the order number and the payment reference — we will check the payment and confirm your order manually.
