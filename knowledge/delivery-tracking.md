# Delivery Tracking

## How to track your order

1. Log in to your account and open **My Orders**.
2. Click **Track Package** next to your order to see live tracking.
3. You can also track with the tracking number on the carrier's website.

## Tracking has not moved

- It is normal for tracking to stay on **"label created"** for up to **2 business days** while the carrier picks up the parcel.
- If tracking has not moved for more than **5 business days**, contact support with your order number and we will open a carrier investigation.

## Delivery times

- Standard delivery: **3–5 business days**.
- Express delivery: **1–2 business days**.
- Deliveries can be delayed by 1–2 days during holidays or bad weather.

## Changing your delivery address

- You can change the delivery address **before the order ships** — contact support as soon as possible with the order number and the new address.
- Once the order has shipped, the address cannot be changed; you can ask the carrier to hold the package for pickup instead.
