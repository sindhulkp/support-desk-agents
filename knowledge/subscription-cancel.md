# Cancelling Your Subscription

## How to cancel

1. Log in to your account and go to **Settings → Subscription**.
2. Click **Cancel Subscription** and confirm.
3. Your subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period — you will not be charged again.

## Refunds on cancellation

- Cancelling stops future charges. It does **not** automatically refund the current month.
- If you were charged in the last **7 days** and have not used the subscription, contact support and we can refund that charge.

## Pausing instead of cancelling

- You can pause your subscription for up to **3 months** instead of cancelling.
- While paused, you are not charged and your account stays saved.

## Double charges on a subscription

- If you see two charges for the same subscription in one month, contact support with the last 4 digits of your card.
- Duplicate charges are refunded within **3–5 business days**.
