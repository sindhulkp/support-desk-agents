Support Desk Agents

A multi-agent customer support desk. Three AI agents handle each ticket: one classifies it, one finds the right help articles, one drafts a grounded reply — and the desk knows when to hand a ticket to a human instead of guessing.

Checking service…

The problem

When a customer writes in, someone has to read the message, decide what kind of problem it is and how urgent, dig through help articles for the answer, and write a reply. Done by hand that is slow and inconsistent — and the reply depends on who happens to pick the ticket up.

1. ClassifierReads the ticket and decides its type, urgency, and the customer’s mood.
2. RetrieverSearches the help articles and pulls the three most relevant ones. If nothing really matches, it says so.
3. ResponderDrafts a reply using only those articles, with citations — or says “I don’t know” and flags it for a human.

What makes it different: a measured relevance threshold (10.0) so weak matches never produce an answer; memory that is actually used — repeat customers are recognised and repeat unhappy ones are escalated; and everything is measured — 20 tests, eval 9/9 classification, 9/9 retrieval, 1/1 honesty.
